Content moderation classifier, with a fairness check
Problem
A single accuracy number can't say where to draw the line, how much lands on human reviewers, or whether a model is fair.
Approach
Trained a TF-IDF baseline and DistilBERT on 1.8 million public comments, set two threshold policies, simulated a review queue and checked false-positive rates by identity group.
Result
ROC-AUC 0.957; 36.6% of toxic comments auto-actioned at ≥ 90% precision; harmless comments wrongly flagged cut from 12.8% to 9.6%.

Dublin bikes + weather data pipeline
Problem
The Dublinbikes feed only shows right now; questions about weather and time of day need history that can be trusted.
Approach
A collector every 15 minutes, then a daily dbt + DuckDB run into an incremental star schema with 14 automatic data tests and Telegram alerts.
Result
Every station, every 15 minutes, modelled and tested into a live dashboard and public dataset, for €0.

Ask the EU Digital Services Act
Problem
People ask about the EU Digital Services Act in everyday words, while the law uses its own terms across 93 articles.
Approach
Split the official text into 482 chunks and compared keyword, semantic and hybrid search on 25 hand-written questions; the demo quotes the law only.
Result
The right article is in the top 3 for 84% of test questions (72% in the free browser version).

SkillBot: from job title to the skills it needs
Problem
Job ads and course catalogues live apart, so "what do I need to learn for this job?" is usually guesswork.
Approach
MSc research on an LLM plus a knowledge graph, and a free in-browser rebuild that matches a job title to similar public job ads.
Result
About 5 of 10 suggested skills appear in the real ad, in a leave-one-out test on 1,167 ads (48%).

Irish rents, 2008–2025: an open dataset, cleaned
Problem
Ireland's official rent table has 674,352 rows, codes repeated as labels and 70% empty cells.
Approach
A reproducible Python cleaning pipeline with a Kaggle notebook that rebuilds the data from the official source.
Result
201,912 rents for 437 places, 2008–2025, published on Kaggle and Hugging Face.

Cross-posting by hand to four sites is slow, but nothing should go public under my name without me seeing it first.

Employment Permits 2025 dashboard
Problem
International students in Ireland rarely know which employers sponsor work permits, or when hiring peaks.
Approach
Cleaned the official 2025 permits dataset, grouped companies into sectors and built a single-page Chart.js dashboard.
Result
A searchable, serverless dashboard of permits by sector, month and employer.
