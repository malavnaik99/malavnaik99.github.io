A free guide to the official pages to check for each immigration stamp. Your stamp is only part of the answer: whether you can get a payment or service usually also depends on your PRSI contributions, the Habitual Residence Condition, a means test, or the conditions of your permission. That's why this guide points you to the official page to check, rather than telling you what you can get.
