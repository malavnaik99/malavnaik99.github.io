**Subject:** Rainy hours had 34% fewer Dublin Bikes pickups (16–31 October 2026)

Hi,

I'm Malav, a data engineer in Dublin. I keep an open record of every Dublinbikes station, every 15 minutes, alongside Met Éireann rain data. Three things from 16–31 October 2026:

- In the 61 rainy hours, about 34% fewer bikes were picked up than in dry hours at the same time of day.
- Dame Street averaged 81.2% of its docks full, so finding a free dock there was hard.
- Fitzwilliam Square East averaged just 4.5% of its docks holding a bike, so it was usually empty.
