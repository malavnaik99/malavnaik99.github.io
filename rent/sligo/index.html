Rent in Sligo

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Sligo was €1,180 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €123 (12%) on a year earlier, and up €414 (54%) on ten years earlier. Sligo is the 18th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Sligo, that rent takes about 44% of take-home pay.
