Irish Rent Reality Check / Kildare

Average rent in Kildare (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kildare was €1,719 in July–December 2025, up €109 (7%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Kildare is the 3rd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €761 (79%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Kildare, the average rent takes about 57% of take-home pay.
