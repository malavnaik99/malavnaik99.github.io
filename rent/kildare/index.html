Irish Rent Reality Check / Kildare

Rent in Kildare

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kildare was €1,719 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €109 (7%) on a year earlier, and up €761 (79%) on ten years earlier. Kildare is the 3rd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Kildare, that rent takes about 57% of take-home pay.
