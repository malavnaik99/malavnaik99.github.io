Irish Rent Reality Check / Roscommon

Average rent in Roscommon (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Roscommon was €1,103 in July–December 2025, up €96 (10%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Roscommon is the 22nd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €629 (133%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Roscommon, the average rent takes about 41% of take-home pay.
