Irish Rent Reality Check / Roscommon

Rent in Roscommon

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Roscommon was €1,103 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €96 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €629 (133%) on ten years earlier. Roscommon is the 22nd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Roscommon, that rent takes about 41% of take-home pay.
