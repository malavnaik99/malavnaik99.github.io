Irish Rent Reality Check / Waterford

Average rent in Waterford (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Waterford was €1,266 in July–December 2025, up €120 (10%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Waterford is the 11th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €683 (117%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Waterford, the average rent takes about 47% of take-home pay.
