Irish Rent Reality Check / Waterford

Rent in Waterford

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Waterford was €1,266 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €120 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €683 (117%) on ten years earlier. Waterford is the 11th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Waterford, that rent takes about 47% of take-home pay.
