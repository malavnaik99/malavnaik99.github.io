Irish Rent Reality Check / Monaghan

Rent in Monaghan

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Monaghan was €1,034 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €109 (12%) on a year earlier, and up €521 (102%) on ten years earlier. Monaghan is the 24th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Monaghan, that rent takes about 41% of take-home pay.
