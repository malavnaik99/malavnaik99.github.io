Irish Rent Reality Check / Carlow

Rent in Carlow

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Carlow was €1,337 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €125 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €684 (105%) on ten years earlier. Carlow is the 9th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Carlow, that rent takes about 51% of take-home pay.
