Irish Rent Reality Check / Carlow

Average rent in Carlow (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Carlow was €1,337 in July–December 2025, up €125 (10%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Carlow is the 9th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €684 (105%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Carlow, the average rent takes about 51% of take-home pay.
