Irish Rent Reality Check / Cork

Average rent in Cork (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Cork was €1,558 in July–December 2025, up €131 (9%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Cork is the 6th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €732 (89%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Cork, the average rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
