Irish Rent Reality Check / Cork

Rent in Cork

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Cork was €1,558 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €131 (9%) on a year earlier, and up €732 (89%) on ten years earlier. Cork is the 6th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Cork, that rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
