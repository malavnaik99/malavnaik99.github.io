Irish Rent Reality Check / Offaly

Rent in Offaly

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Offaly was €1,210 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €106 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €634 (110%) on ten years earlier. Offaly is the 14th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Offaly, that rent takes about 45% of take-home pay.
