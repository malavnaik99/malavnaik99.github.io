Irish Rent Reality Check / Longford

Rent in Longford

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Longford was €1,134 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €112 (11%) on a year earlier, and up €691 (156%) on ten years earlier. Longford is the 19th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Longford, that rent takes about 45% of take-home pay.
