Irish Rent Reality Check / Meath

Rent in Meath

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Meath was €1,547 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €104 (7%) on a year earlier, and up €723 (88%) on ten years earlier. Meath is the 7th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Meath, that rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
