Irish Rent Reality Check / Meath

Average rent in Meath (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Meath was €1,547 in July–December 2025, up €104 (7%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Meath is the 7th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €723 (88%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Meath, the average rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
