Irish Rent Reality Check / Leitrim

Rent in Leitrim

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Leitrim was €1,010 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €93 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €571 (130%) on ten years earlier. Leitrim is the 25th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Leitrim, that rent takes about 39% of take-home pay.
