Irish Rent Reality Check / Leitrim

Average rent in Leitrim (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Leitrim was €1,010 in July–December 2025, up €93 (10%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Leitrim is the 25th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €571 (130%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Leitrim, the average rent takes about 39% of take-home pay.

Is rent going up in Leitrim?
