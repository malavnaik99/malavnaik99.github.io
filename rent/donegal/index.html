Rent in Donegal

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Donegal was €992 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €88 (10%) on a year earlier, and up €530 (115%) on ten years earlier. Donegal is the 26th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Donegal, that rent takes about 42% of take-home pay.
