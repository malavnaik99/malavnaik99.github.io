Irish Rent Reality Check / Wicklow

Rent in Wicklow

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Wicklow was €1,739 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €97 (6%) on a year earlier, and up €780 (81%) on ten years earlier. Wicklow is the 2nd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Wicklow, that rent takes about 61% of take-home pay.
