Irish Rent Reality Check / Wicklow

Average rent in Wicklow (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Wicklow was €1,739 in July–December 2025, up €97 (6%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Wicklow is the 2nd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €780 (81%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Wicklow, the average rent takes about 61% of take-home pay.
