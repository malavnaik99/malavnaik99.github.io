Irish Rent Reality Check / Tipperary

Average rent in Tipperary (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Tipperary was €1,092 in July–December 2025, up €104 (11%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Tipperary is the 23rd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €539 (97%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Tipperary, the average rent takes about 41% of take-home pay.
