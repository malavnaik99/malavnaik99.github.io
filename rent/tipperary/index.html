Irish Rent Reality Check / Tipperary

Rent in Tipperary

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Tipperary was €1,092 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €104 (11%) on a year earlier, and up €539 (97%) on ten years earlier. Tipperary is the 23rd most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Tipperary, that rent takes about 41% of take-home pay.
