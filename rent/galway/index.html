Irish Rent Reality Check / Galway

Rent in Galway

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Galway was €1,654 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €139 (9%) on a year earlier, and up €829 (100%) on ten years earlier. Galway is the 4th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Galway, that rent takes about 60% of take-home pay.
