Irish Rent Reality Check / Galway

Average rent in Galway (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Galway was €1,654 in July–December 2025, up €139 (9%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Galway is the 4th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €829 (100%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Galway, the average rent takes about 60% of take-home pay.
