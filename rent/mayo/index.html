Average rent in Mayo (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Mayo was €1,127 in July–December 2025, up €116 (11%) on a year earlier.

Mayo is the 20th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €598 (113%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Mayo, the average rent takes about 44% of take-home pay.
