Irish Rent Reality Check / Mayo

Rent in Mayo

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Mayo was €1,127 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €116 (11%) on a year earlier, and up €598 (113%) on ten years earlier. Mayo is the 20th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Mayo, that rent takes about 44% of take-home pay.
