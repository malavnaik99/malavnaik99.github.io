Irish Rent Reality Check / Wexford

Rent in Wexford

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Wexford was €1,192 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €79 (7%) on a year earlier, and up €605 (103%) on ten years earlier. Wexford is the 16th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Wexford, that rent takes about 47% of take-home pay.
