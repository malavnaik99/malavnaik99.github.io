Irish Rent Reality Check / Wexford

Average rent in Wexford (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Wexford was €1,192 in July–December 2025, up €79 (7%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Wexford is the 16th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €605 (103%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Wexford, the average rent takes about 47% of take-home pay.
