Irish Rent Reality Check / Kerry

Average rent in Kerry (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kerry was €1,201 in July–December 2025, up €100 (9%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Kerry is the 15th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €615 (105%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Kerry, the average rent takes about 49% of take-home pay.

Frequently asked questions

Is rent going up in Kerry?

Is Kerry cheaper than Cork?

Yes. The average new rent is €1,201 a month in Kerry and €1,558 in Cork, so Kerry is €357 a month cheaper (July–December 2025).
