Irish Rent Reality Check / Kerry

Rent in Kerry

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kerry was €1,201 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €100 (9%) on a year earlier, and up €615 (105%) on ten years earlier. Kerry is the 15th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Kerry, that rent takes about 49% of take-home pay.
