Irish Rent Reality Check / Limerick

Average rent in Limerick (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Limerick was €1,651 in July–December 2025, up €197 (14%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Limerick is the 5th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €979 (146%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Limerick, the average rent takes about 59% of take-home pay.
