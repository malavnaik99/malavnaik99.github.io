Irish Rent Reality Check / Limerick

Rent in Limerick

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Limerick was €1,651 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €197 (14%) on a year earlier, and up €979 (146%) on ten years earlier. Limerick is the 5th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Limerick, that rent takes about 59% of take-home pay.
