Irish Rent Reality Check / Cavan

Rent in Cavan

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Cavan was €1,111 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €111 (11%) on a year earlier, and up €631 (131%) on ten years earlier. Cavan is the 21st most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Cavan, that rent takes about 42% of take-home pay.
