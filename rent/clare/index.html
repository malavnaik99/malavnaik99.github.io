Irish Rent Reality Check / Clare

Rent in Clare

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Clare was €1,182 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €91 (8%) on a year earlier, and up €631 (115%) on ten years earlier. Clare is the 17th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Clare, that rent takes about 43% of take-home pay.
