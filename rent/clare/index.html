Irish Rent Reality Check / Clare

Average rent in Clare (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Clare was €1,182 in July–December 2025, up €91 (8%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Clare is the 17th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €631 (115%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Clare, the average rent takes about 43% of take-home pay.
