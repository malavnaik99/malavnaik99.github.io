Irish Rent Reality Check / Dublin

Rent in Dublin

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Dublin was €2,162 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €88 (4%) on a year earlier, and up €869 (67%) on ten years earlier. Dublin is the most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Dublin, that rent takes about 71% of take-home pay.
