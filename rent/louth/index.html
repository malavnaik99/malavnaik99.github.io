Irish Rent Reality Check / Louth

Average rent in Louth (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Louth was €1,446 in July–December 2025, up €118 (9%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05) · Licence CC BY 4.0

Louth is the 8th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €730 (102%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Louth, the average rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
