Irish Rent Reality Check / Louth

Rent in Louth

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Louth was €1,446 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €118 (9%) on a year earlier, and up €730 (102%) on ten years earlier. Louth is the 8th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Louth, that rent takes about 54% of take-home pay.
