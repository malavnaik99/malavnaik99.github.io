Irish Rent Reality Check / Kilkenny

Rent in Kilkenny

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kilkenny was €1,216 in July–December 2025, for all homes. That is up €88 (8%) on a year earlier, and up €557 (85%) on ten years earlier. Kilkenny is the 13th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents. On typical pay in Co. Kilkenny, that rent takes about 44% of take-home pay.
