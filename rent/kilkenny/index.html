Irish Rent Reality Check / Kilkenny

Average rent in Kilkenny (2025)

The average monthly rent for a new tenancy in County Kilkenny was €1,216 in July–December 2025, up €88 (8%) on a year earlier.

Data last updated: 14 May 2026 · Source: RTB via CSO (RIH02), earnings CSO (DEA05)

Kilkenny is the 13th most expensive of Ireland's 26 counties for new rents, and rents are up €557 (85%) on ten years earlier. On typical pay in Co. Kilkenny, the average rent takes about 44% of take-home pay.
