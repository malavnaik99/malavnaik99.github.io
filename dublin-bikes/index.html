How these numbers are made

  • Pickups are estimates. Every 15 minutes the pipeline records how many bikes each station has. When a station has fewer bikes than at the previous snapshot, the difference counts as pickups. Bikes taken away by the rebalancing vans count too, so this is not an official trip count.
  • Gaps: a drop across a gap of more than 30 minutes (missed snapshots) is not counted. Stations with no capacity, or that never had a bike in the period, are left out.
  • Docks holding a bike: the average number of available bikes divided by the station's capacity.
  • Rain: a rainy hour has at least 0.1 mm of rain at Dublin Airport (Met Éireann); a dry hour has none. Hours with only a trace (0.01 mm) or no reading are left out. Rainy hours are compared with the dry-hour average at the same hour of day, weekdays and weekends matched separately.
  • Times are Irish local time.
