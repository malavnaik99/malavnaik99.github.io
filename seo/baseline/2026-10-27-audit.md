## Top issues
1. **`/permits-2025/` (static HTML)**: no meta description, no Open Graph image, and the title has a typo, "Permits 2025 | Verifed Analytics", with a brand that isn't yours. This is the page most likely to be shared from Irish open-data showcases.
2. **Meta descriptions too long** on the home page (213 chars) and 6 of 8 project pages (180–233 chars). Google cuts them at about 155–160.
3. **`/contact/` has no internal links** pointing to it. Nothing in the nav or footer links there, so it is close to orphaned.

## Other findings
- Post titles plus " | Malav Naik" run 68–113 chars, so Google will truncate them. That's normal for blog posts and not urgent, but shorter `title` values would help the long ones.
- Short meta descriptions: `/blog/topics/` (53), `/contact/` (60), `/now/` (61).
- 16 photos in `/images/` are 200–520 KB JPEGs. WebP or resizing would help LCP on `/about/`.
- `sameAs` in Person includes the MSc thesis URL, which is a document, not a profile of you.
